Benchmarking DeepSeek, Qwen & Gemma on Consumer Hardware

May 18, 2026 · 12 min read

The open-weight LLM landscape has evolved rapidly. Models that were impossible to run locally two years ago now perform remarkably well on consumer GPUs. This post presents a systematic comparison of three leading model families — DeepSeek, Qwen, and Gemma — running on my homelab hardware via Ollama.

Test Methodology

All benchmarks were conducted on the same hardware: a single NVIDIA RTX 4090 with 24GB VRAM. Each model was tested at its highest feasible quantization level, measuring tokens per second for both prompt processing and generation, VRAM utilization, and time-to-first-token latency.

Models Tested

  • DeepSeek-V3 (various parameter counts, Q4_K_M quantization)
  • Qwen-2.5 (7B, 14B, 32B, 72B variants)
  • Gemma 2 (9B, 27B variants)

Key Findings

The results were revealing. Qwen-2.5 72B delivered the best quality-per-VRAM ratio at Q4_K_M quantization, while Gemma 2 27B offered the best speed for its parameter class. DeepSeek excelled at code generation tasks specifically.

Practical Recommendations

For general-purpose local inference with a single 24GB GPU, Qwen-2.5 32B at Q5_K_M offers the best balance of quality and speed. For code-specific workflows, DeepSeek models remain competitive. Gemma 2 is the clear winner for latency-sensitive applications where faster responses matter more than maximum capability.

Full benchmark data and scripts are available in my GitHub repository.
